📂 1. Transportation & Logistics Networks
Focuses on global infrastructure hubs, transit pipelines, and macro-routing patterns.

Examples: The Global Airports Map (your current script), undersea fiber-optic telecommunication cables, global shipping lanes, continental railway networks.

Why it matters: Demonstrates Lonboard’s ability to draw high-density linestrings and point arrays that spans across hemispheres without visual distortion.

📂 2. Real-Time Urban Mobility & Micro-Transit
Captures high-frequency, fast-moving city dynamics.

Examples: City-wide e-scooter/bikeshare GPS drop-offs, ride-share trajectory flows, real-time bus/metro fleet locations.

Why it matters: Highlights how Lonboard handles dynamic, high-cadence data streams that change minute-by-minute.

📂 3. 3D Digital Twins & Urban Planning
Focuses on structural geometries, building density, and volumetric analysis.

Examples: 3D extruded city building heights (like the DuckDB + Overture workflow), zoning parcels, structural shadows, floor-area ratios.

Why it matters: Showcases the library’s Z-axis capabilities, pitch/bearing rendering adjustments, and spatial extrusion performance.

📂 4. Climate Risk & Natural Hazards
Tracks massive, unstructured environmental shapes and emergency boundaries.

Examples: Historical wildfire burn perimeters, flood zone inundation maps, sea-level rise projections, earthquake fault lines.

Why it matters: Proves that complex multi-polygons with messy internal holes or overlapping edges render smoothly on the GPU.

📂 5. Conservation & Ecological Tracking
Deals with continuous tracking pathways, biomass distribution, and nature boundaries.

Examples: Real-time wildlife migration corridors, global forest cover loss zones, protected marine sanctuaries, bird flight trajectories.

Why it matters: Perfect for highlighting the PathLayer and specialized timeline/animation tracking extensions.

📂 6. Telecommunications & Utility Infrastructure
Visualizes dense, hyper-local linear networks and grid layouts.

Examples: Power grid transmission lines, cellular tower coverage drop zones, municipal water main networks, broadband coverage holes.

Why it matters: Highlights massive spatial join results where millions of tiny domestic lines must stay crisp at high zoom levels.

📂 7. Socio-Demographic & Public Health Analytics
Explores human patterns, density metrics, and societal disparities.

Examples: High-resolution population density grids, air pollution/AQI tracking networks, healthcare accessibility polygons, census tract income distributions.

Why it matters: Demonstrates advanced thematic styling, dynamic classification breaks, and smooth continuous color mapping.

📂 8. Energy Resources & Extraction Footprints
Tracks physical extraction sites and sustainable infrastructure distribution.

Examples: Global wind farm turbine coordinates, oil/gas pipeline networks, solar panel roof installations, lithium mining perimeters.

Why it matters: Combines high-density point datasets (turbines) with structural polygon footprints seamlessly.

📂 9. Agricultural Systems & Food Security
Deals with land-surface classification, crop health arrays, and water management.

Examples: National crop type parcel maps, satellite-derived vegetation indices (NDVI) converted to vector shapes, precision irrigation center-pivots.

Why it matters: Explores the boundary limits of massive parcel mapping datasets that usually cause desktop GIS software to lag or crash.

📂 10. Oceanography & Hydrographic Modeling
Focuses on maritime depths, river systems, and moving water paths.

Examples: High-density stream networks (Strahler stream orders), oceanic bathymetric depth contours, ocean current velocity fields.

Why it matters: Shifts the focus off land masses to display Lonboard's strength in styling dark-mode bathymetric surfaces and heavy line matrices.
